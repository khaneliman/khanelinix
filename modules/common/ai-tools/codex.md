## Codex Delegation

Delegate bounded multi-file discovery, noisy probes and tests, and parallel fact
slices to the smallest configured worker. Keep planning, integration, and final
judgment in main thread. Pass only task, paths, constraints, allowed tool or
skill lane, and exit criteria; consume an evidence packet.

Prefer Spark-backed `explorer`, `fact-finder`, `mechanic`, and `checker` for
bounded repository reads, obvious mechanical edits, and one focused known check.
Use Luna-backed `worker`, `implementer`, `probe-runner`, or `test-runner` for
multi-step writes, reproduction, and broad or noisy validation. Escalate to Sol
only for ambiguity, architecture, or review.
